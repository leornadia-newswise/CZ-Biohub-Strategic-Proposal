Options to Increase Results

What we recommend next: CZ Biohub has strong results when active — but publishing has been intermittent. The fastest path to more coverage is a consistent cadence (1–2 stories/month), paired with targeted pitching and expert visibility.

Content ideas already present on CZ Biohub’s site that can be distributed and pitched include research updates, interviews/podcasts, tool/resource announcements, partnerships, and program highlights.

01

Publish More (and More Consistently)

Increase total story volume by moving from “occasional launches” to a repeatable monthly publishing rhythm. Consistency is the #1 lever that increases outlet engagement.

  • Recommendation: 12–18 stories/year (1–2 per month)
  • Best-fit content: research milestones, tools/resources, partnerships, events, programs
  • Distribution upgrade: expand targeting by science/health beats + outlet types
What we would do with you Monthly editorial calendar Targeted journalist lists Embargo-ready distribution
02

Use Analytics to Optimize What Works

Go beyond “views” and turn each release into a learning loop: which beats engage, which outlets respond, and what content types drive the strongest pickup and clips.

  • Recommendation: monthly performance snapshot + quarterly trend review
  • Identify: top outlet categories + repeat engager lists
  • Improve: headlines, summaries, and targeting based on performance
What we would do with you Outlet & beat tracking Engagement reports Content testing & iteration
03

Expert Database (Yes—Pitching Experts)

Position CZ Biohub researchers as go-to sources for breaking news and explainers. This is a separate pathway to visibility that doesn’t depend on publishing a full release.

  • Recommendation: build 10–20 expert profiles aligned to key beats
  • Pitching: respond to media inquiries + proactive “expert availability” outreach
  • Thought leadership: rapid comments on timely topics (AI, genomics, cancer, etc.)
What we would do with you Expert profile creation Inquiry matching Proactive pitching support
04

Strategic Support (Done-With-You Plan)

If CZ Biohub wants more consistent results without extra workload, we can build and run a repeatable comms plan: topics, timing, packaging, and audience targeting.

  • Recommendation: 90-day launch plan + 6-month editorial roadmap
  • Message and content optimization (clear, reporter-friendly summaries)
  • Timing recommendations (conference/journal/embargo coordination)
What we would do with you Planning + editorial support Audience targeting Quarterly review & reset

Recommended Quick-Start (Next 60 Days)

Step 1: Confirm 6–8 story topics already on your calendar (research milestones, tool launches, interviews).
Step 2: Publish 1 story every 2–4 weeks with targeted lists (AI, genomics, cancer, tools).
